Connecticut Winters Are Hard on Garage Doors, Here's Why That Matters to You

If there's one thing that defines garage door repair calls in Enfield, it's the brutal effect of New England's freeze-thaw cycle on garage door hardware. Springs that have been quietly wearing out all year, accumulating cycles, gradually fatiguing, tend to finally let go on the coldest morning of a cold snap, because cold steel is measurably more brittle than steel at room temperature. Cables corrode faster with road salt tracked in on tires and boots all winter. Openers that ran fine through October start struggling by January, as cold temperatures thicken lubricant and put extra strain on aging gears and motors. This is exactly why our phones ring the most from late November through February, and it's exactly why we built our whole approach around understanding this pattern rather than treating every call as a one-off mystery.
